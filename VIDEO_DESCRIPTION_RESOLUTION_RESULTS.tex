\noindent
All experiments use the same 80 paired videos, prompts, and references. High is the original
1920$\times$1080 video and Low is the corresponding 432$\times$240 version. Both models
uniformly sample 24 frames at matching temporal positions. Qwen3.5-9B is excluded because
the same 24-frame setting exceeds the available GPU memory; 12-frame results are retained
in the experiment notes as supplementary results.

\medskip
\noindent
GPT-5.1 VDD independently scores each description from 0 to 5. Pairwise evaluation compares
High and Low anonymously in both A/B orders; only agreement across both orders counts as a
win. High win rate excludes Tie/conflict cases.

\noindent
The VDC experiment uses 214 paired 1920$\times$1080 and 432$\times$240 videos, with
24 uniformly sampled frames and 4,113 detailed QA pairs per quality. A local
Llama-3.1-8B judge applies the two-stage VDCScore procedure. Reported $p$ values are from
two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests over per-video High--Low differences.

\medskip
\noindent
Qwen3-VL-4B shows a statistically significant but small High-resolution advantage.
Qwen3.5-4B has smaller, non-significant gaps. Because a small portion of the provided VDC
QA references is malformed or overly generic, absolute scores should be interpreted
cautiously; the paired High--Low differences are the main comparison.

\medskip
\noindent
High resolution is generally favored, but the effect varies by model. The largest VDD gap
appears for Qwen3-VL-4B with 24 frames, which is consistent with its pairwise result. The
judge compares generated descriptions with reference text and does not view the videos.
